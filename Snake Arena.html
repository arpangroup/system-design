Snake Arena

Single player, local two-player, or an AI opponent at three strengths — a greedy pathfinder, a Hamiltonian cycle that mathematically can't trap itself, and a Q-learning agent you can train from scratch. Scores, leaderboards, and a prize-bearing high-score contest all persist on this device.

Player one steers with the arrow keys. Player two (when active) steers with W A S D.

Self-play training

Trains a Q-table over a compact 11-feature state (danger ahead/left/right, current heading, food direction) rather than the raw board — small enough to learn exactly, and it generalizes across every raw board sharing those features. Every 10% of the run it freezes the policy and measures average food eaten over 20 evaluation games.
